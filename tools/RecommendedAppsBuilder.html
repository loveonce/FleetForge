Quick Guide for Team & Freshers

This tool creates the official recommended_apps.json file used by FleetForge. You can add any custom software, partner tools, or affiliate packages:

  • Category Dropdown: Select any of the 9 official FleetForge categories.
  • Download URL: Direct link to .exe or .msi. FleetForge downloads it behind the scenes (URL is completely hidden from end users).
  • Silent Arguments: Click any of the convenient preset pills (e.g., /S or /VERYSILENT /NORESTART) so installation runs automatically without popups.
